Every part of the Trading Agents desk, one test at a time, on your own computer: the team in Madav's Agent Studio, the analyses, the dashboard and your first month on it, your Robinhood accounts, deciding on a call, the paper record and the guard rails, placing an order, care for what you hold, the routines and the safety rules. New to the desk?
